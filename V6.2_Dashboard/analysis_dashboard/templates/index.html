Hover a chart to read every signal at that moment.

Solid lines are real readings (the average of each step, with the band showing its lowest and highest). Dashed stretches are estimates: a straight line between readings, a level carried forward, or no sip in that step. Nothing is drawn across a pause of more than a minute. The CSV has the same values, plus a .n column per signal: how many real readings went into each row (0 = estimate).

For ReaLLM-Forge downloads a zip for data/csv_mc_int: the same signals as whole numbers (decimals kept by scaling, e.g. temp_c_x10), the time as minute_of_day and second, and -1 (one below each column's range) where a device wasn't recording. Inside: the CSV, a .columns.json for turning samples back into real units (tools/mcint_decode.py) and a .prepare.sh that runs get_dataset.sh with every --range. Uses the step shown.
